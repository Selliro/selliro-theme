Whispo is a premium Shopify theme for beauty, skincare and lifestyle brands, with storytelling sections, bundles,
quizzes, 3D products and built-in wholesale pricing, all editable in the theme editor without apps.

### Added
- Important: Wholesale / B2B section with a volume-pricing calculator, margin at retail price and a quote form. Business customers on Shopify B2B see their own contract pricing.
- Important: Bundle builder that lets shoppers fill a box and unlock tiered discounts.
- Important: 3D product viewer with feature callouts, camera views and View in your space (AR).
- Slideshow with a floating product image that travels into New Arrivals as shoppers scroll.
- Routine builder and skin quiz page that recommend products.
- Product story, timeline, before / after slider and reveal image sections for storytelling.
- New Arrivals showcase with quick view and quick add, Shop by category grid and shop-the-look bundles.
- Conversion-focused product page: ratings, social proof, pair-with upsell, ingredients, routine steps, product facts, comparison table and a sticky add-to-cart bar.
- Contact page with contact cards, form and map; Theme features page; Our Story page.
- Animated footer wordmark and announcement bar with icons.
